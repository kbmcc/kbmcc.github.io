READING THE DATA

A useful comparison, with a clear limit.

The OpenElections 2022 precinct file has major-party gubernatorial results for 101 counties. This comparison uses the 97 counties with usable Republican and Democratic totals in both 2018 and 2022—not a complete statewide count. Other candidates are excluded when calculating the two-party margin.

Precinct results are summed by county. Margin is (Republican votes − Democratic votes) ÷ (Republican votes + Democratic votes); swing is the 2022 margin minus the 2018 margin. The median gives each county equal weight.
